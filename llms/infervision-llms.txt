# Infervision

> Infervision is a medical artificial intelligence company that builds AI-powered medical imaging and clinical decision-support software for hospitals and healthcare providers. Its InferRead diagnostic suite and InferOperate surgical-planning suite assist radiologists and surgeons across CT/DR reading, triage, and 3D reconstruction. The company holds FDA 510(k) clearance for InferRead CT Lung and operates across 30+ countries. No public developer API is currently published.

## Products
- InferRead: Diagnostic AI for CT Lung, DR Chest, CT Coronary, CT Stroke, CT Bone, and CT Pneumonia.
- InferOperate: 3D reconstruction and surgical planning for thoracic, liver, and urological procedures.
- InferCare: Patient management and image follow-up.
- InferScholar: AI-enabled medical research platform.
- InferMatrix: AI-enabled medical application platform.
